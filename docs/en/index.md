# Physics Beyond Classroom

Physics Beyond Classroom is a physics website written by a high school student. It focuses on electromagnetic radiation and how it interacts with matter.

The site grew out of three research experiences: hydrogen frequency standards, semiconductor spectroscopy, and gamma-ray astronomy. Each project had its own final paper or report, but along the way I also met many interesting physics ideas that did not make it into the final results. I wanted to understand them better and keep them organized, so I collected them here.

The articles start from high-school physics and work toward the real instruments and methods used in research, with intuitive explanations, diagrams, and the derivations that matter. The three topics are organized by the part of the electromagnetic spectrum they belong to — microwaves, visible and ultraviolet light, and gamma rays. They are different questions; the spectrum is simply a convenient way to arrange them.
